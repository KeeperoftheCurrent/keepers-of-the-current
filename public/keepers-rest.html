Talia’s Circle of Truth

This is Talia’s personal power. Within the Circle she creates, subjects are compelled to give the truth, without lies or omissions. Meetings are hosted by Talia herself.

Seek the Keeper in person

Approach Talia Emberborn at Keepers Rest and bring your request before her in character. Tell her who seeks the table and what matter you wish to resolve. Arrangements are made with Talia in person.
