The outreach program of the Current

Keepers Rest

A welcome beneath the maroon and gold. A place for connection.

Our main gathering area welcomes everyone

Come meet the Keepers, share a conversation, or discover the Current. You do not need to be a member to visit our main area.

Keepers Rest is the outreach program of the Current. We belong to no Kingdom, and Independents are warmly welcome alongside those who carry a Kingdom’s banner.

A little sanctuary for our members

Members of the Current’s faith have their own private spaces and daily provisions beyond the shared gathering area.

Coffee & breakfast

Members get coffee and breakfast every morning.

A place to rest

A separate, private area for members to sit down and rest, away from the main gathering space.

A place for belongings

A separate, members-only area to store personal belongings.

A full kitchen

Members have a full kitchen to use, including a small oven.

Come say hello

Look for our maroon-and-gold pavilion and the Keepers Rest sign. One of the Current’s leaders can welcome you and tell you more about visiting or becoming a member.

You do not need to join the faith to try Level 1 trials: they are open to all Hynafol members.
