The outreach program of the Current

Keepers Rest

A welcome for independents. A place for connection.

We belong to no Kingdom

The Current is an independent faith. Keepers Rest is our outreach program, where independents are welcome.

You are welcome to meet the Keepers and learn about the Current. Access to our members’ spaces is reserved for members.

Discover what the Current means →

For members

Coffee & breakfast

Members get coffee and breakfast every morning.

A place to rest

A dedicated, members-only area to sit down and rest.

A place for belongings

A separate, members-only area to store personal belongings.

A full kitchen

Members have a full kitchen to use, including a small oven.

Kitchen use for non-members

Non-members can negotiate kitchen use with one of the Current’s leaders. Please speak with a leader before using the kitchen.

The rest area and personal-belongings storage area are strictly members-only. Permission to use the kitchen does not include access to those areas.

Not sure where to begin?

Speak with one of the Current’s leaders about membership, visiting Keepers Rest, or arranging kitchen use.

You do not need to join the faith to try Level 1 trials: they are open to all Hynafol members.
