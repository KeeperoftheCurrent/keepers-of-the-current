An independent faith

Find your place in the Circle

You may walk independently or carry another banner. The Current belongs to no Kingdom; your own affiliations need not cost you a home here.

To learn about joining the faith, approach Talia or one of the Current’s leaders at Keepers Rest. Come in character, bring your questions, and share what draws you to the Circle.

Visiting Keepers Rest and walking Level 1 trials do not require faith membership. Level 1 welcomes all Hynafol denizens.
